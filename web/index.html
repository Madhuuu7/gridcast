gridcast

Day-ahead load forecasting for the Delhi grid, on three years of real 5-minute SLDC readings. Every forecast is for all 96 fifteen-minute scheduling blocks of the next day, issued at the 10:00 gate, and every interval's coverage is measured rather than assumed.
